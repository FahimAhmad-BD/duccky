# Duccky v1.0: Mechanical Design

**Document:** DCK-03 · **Revision:** draft · **Phase:** 3 · **Status:** In progress

## 1. How the CAD is built

Fusion is the CAD master, and every part is generated by Python scripts that Claude runs inside Fusion through the Claude Fusion Bridge add-in. The scripts live in `cad/lib` (geometry) and `cad/build` (jobs). No part is drawn by hand, so the whole robot can be rebuilt from the repository, and every dimension traces to a line of code with its source.

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| `servo.py` | ROBOTIS X330 reference body from the ROBOTIS drawing (28 May 2020): case 20 × 34 × 23 mm, horn Ø16 × 3 mm on a 12 mm pitch circle of M2 holes, axis 9.5 mm from the horn end, M2 corner holes 16 × 30 mm on both faces; mass set to the e-Manual figure (18 or 23 g) |
| `prim.py`, `dk.py` | Boxes, cylinders, joins and cuts; materials; physical properties |
| `leg.py`, `legparts.py` | Leg layout and the five printed leg parts; knee spring |
| `robot.py`, `trunk.py`, `cartridge.py` | Robot frame, trunk frame, battery cartridge, carrier board placement |
| `head.py`, `headparts.py` | Neck and head layout and parts, beak |
| `assembly.py` | Whole robot, with every part tagged by kinematic link |

Jobs: `leg_build.py`, `head_build.py` and `trunk_check.py` build an area and run Fusion's interference analysis at the zero pose and across each joint's range. `export_robot.py` writes `cad/export/robot.json` (parts with mass properties and link, joints with frames and limits). `show_robot.py` and `save_master.py` build the whole robot for viewing or saving.

## 2. Design rules

1. **One servo pocket.** Every servo sits in a printed pocket and is fixed through the two far end corner holes on each face with countersunk M2 screws, so no head stands proud. Pocket walls occupy 11.5 to 13.5 mm from the servo mid plane.
2. **U outputs on horn and idler.** Every output link has a plate on the horn and a plate on the ROBOTIS FPX330-H101 idler, 4 × M2 on a 12 mm circle each side. Output plates occupy 14.5 to 17.5 mm from the servo mid plane, 1 mm outside the pocket walls.
3. **Swept radius clearance.** A link that turns past a neighbouring servo stays at least 1 mm outside that servo's swept radius about the joint axis (26.5 mm for the X330 far corner). This rule sets the hip roll to pitch offset, the neck link length and the head shell cavity.
4. **Printed material.** PETG (Prusament, 1.27 g/cm³, ISO 1183), 3 mm plates and 2 mm pocket walls. Mass estimates use a 1.2 mm solid skin plus 25 % infill.

## 3. Leg

Arrangement: hip yaw servo vertical in the trunk, horn down; hip roll servo lying horizontal under the yaw horn with its case pointing inward; hip pitch servo hanging below it in the roll bracket; knee servo in the thigh, horn end down; ankle servo lying in the foot. Offsets from `analysis/layout/leg_layout.py`: hip yaw to roll 14.0 mm, roll to pitch 30.7 mm, ankle to sole 13.0 mm; thigh 52 mm and shank 46 mm keep the height near 29.7 cm (DCK-01 sections 10 and 11).

Interference: none at the zero pose or at either limit of hip roll (±0.40 rad), hip pitch (±1.60 rad), knee (−0.10 to 2.20 rad) and ankle (±1.40 rad).

Knee assist spring (RK2 closed): Lee Spring LTL048J 04 or LTR048J 04 in music wire (OD 15.72 mm, 1.22 mm wire, 7.42 mm body, 11.51 mm mandrel), 31.68 kg mm at 180° deflection, so 0.0989 N m/rad, within 1 % of the 0.10 N m/rad the sizing study needs. It sits on the inner side of each knee on a printed mandrel boss, with its legs bearing on a shank peg and on a thigh peg in front of the knee that the shank never reaches.

## 4. Trunk

The hip yaw servos stand on a 2 mm floor (z 1 to 3 mm) and are clamped from above by a 3 mm deck (z 26 to 29 mm) resting on their back faces. The deck bridges over the battery cartridge, which slides in from the rear between the yaw servos, and carries four M2.5 standoffs for the carrier board on the Radxa hole pattern (DCK-02 RK10). The trunk is 96 mm wide, set by the yaw servos at ±34 mm, not the 80 mm of the Phase 0 envelope. The deck, floor and side walls are windowed to save mass (RK1).

Battery cartridge: two Molicel INR-18650-P28A cells lengthwise, in a tray and lid split at the cell centre line, protection board and blade contacts at the front end, finger recess at the back.

## 5. Neck and head

The neck pitch servo is fixed at the trunk's front top, case pointing back over the computer (axis 58 mm above the hip yaw plane). A horizontal 38 mm neck link of two separate side plates carries the head base forward, outside the neck servo's swept radius. A bridge between the plates would meet the board, trunk or head somewhere in the neck's 2.2 rad sweep; the two servos close the frame instead.

The head base holds the head pitch servo (case pointing forward) and, on a 3 mm spacer, the head yaw servo standing with its axis directly above the head pitch axis, clamped by a collar screwed into its four horn face holes. The yaw link carries the head roll servo lying along y. The head shell hangs from the roll horn and idler; its cavity is the roll servo and yaw link envelope swept over ±0.45 rad, and its underside keeps clear of the head base at every yaw and roll pose.

The beak servo stands in the head front, horn end down, with its axis at the head floor. The upper bill is part of the head shell; the lower mandible hangs from the beak horn and idler and opens downward 0 to 0.7 rad. Everything below the beak axis swings back as the beak opens, so the drop arms and the blade start 18 to 27 mm ahead of the axis and reach it through notches in the shell front corners; a sampled clearance search over beak opening combined with head roll set the 0.7 rad limit.

Joint limits from the Fusion clearance search: neck pitch −1.5 to +0.7 rad, head pitch −0.8 to +1.5 rad, head yaw ±2.9 rad, head roll ±0.45 rad, beak 0 to 0.7 rad.

## 6. Mass

From the Fusion export (`cad/export/robot.json`, 38 parts, 15 joints) through `analysis/mass/mass_report.py`. Printed parts use PETG at 1.27 g/cm³ with a 1.2 mm solid skin and 25 % infill; most parts are thin enough to be solid. Servo masses are the e-Manual figures, the cells the retailer figure. Items not yet in CAD keep their DCK-00 and DCK-02 allocations.

| Group | Mass (g) |
|---|---|
| Servos (15) | 300 |
| Printed parts | 267 |
| Allocations (board, computer, sensors, harness, fasteners, idlers, head payload, outer shells) | 165 |
| Cells | 92 |
| Knee springs | 6 |
| **Total** | **831** |

The total is 831 g against the 850 g limit, a 19 g margin. The centre of mass is 17.3 mm ahead of the hip yaw axes, on the centreline and 2.7 mm above the hip yaw horn faces. The heaviest printed parts are the head shell (51.5 g) and the trunk frame (47.5 g).

With these masses in `analysis/sizing/params.py`, configuration G still passes every rule (`analysis/sizing/results/compare.md`). Hip pitch walking demand is 0.362 N m, 38.9 % of the XC330 stall torque against the 40 % limit (39.6 % before the trunk and head were lightened). RK1 stays open as a watch item: any growth in trunk or head mass, the outer shells above all, must be checked with `compare.py` before release.
